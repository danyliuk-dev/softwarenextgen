NextgenReportEngine

Create reports in the installed Nextgen Reports application, or connect your own application to its ready-to-run engine using the included SDK.

Run

Install your platform package, then open Nextgen Reports from Applications or your saved Linux AppImage. Studio and the engine start together. Select Preview, change the sample, and export PDF, HTML, images, Excel or Word. No product compilation is required.

Connect your application

The installed resources include SDK/NuGet with NextgenReports.Client and NextgenReports.Blazor, and SDK/Flutter/nextgen_reports. Copy these into your application's vendor directory. Follow the developer guide to start the installed engine for integration, then .NET/Blazor or Flutter/Dart to connect your app. Rendering runs in the engine, not in the browser or mobile device.
